# Brand logo sources

Donovan supplied every logo on 2026-10-31. Claude traced each one into a
single-color white SVG with a transparent background, cropped tightly.
The supplied files are kept outside the repo, in the git-ignored
`originals/brands/` folder.

| Logo | Supplied file | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| American Red Cross | `ARC.jpeg` (from a clip-art site, fake checkerboard background) | Dropped the 3D ball behind the cross. **Needs permission:** redcross.org/brand says users who aren't authorized need the Red Cross's express permission to use its logo. |
| U.S. Navy | `Navy-Symbol.png` (black on white) | **Needs permission:** use of Navy trademarks goes through the Navy Trademark Licensing Office (navy.mil/TRADEMARKS/Public-Use). |
| Xfinity Mobile | `xfinity-mobile-logo-freelogovectors.net_.png` (freelogovectors.net) | Blue accent made white. |
| Comcast Business | `cb.webp` (clip-art site, fake checkerboard and watermark) | Watermark removed. |
| MTV | `MTV.jpg` (clip-art site, fake checkerboard) | Line version: outlined M, solid "TV". |
| Katy Perry | `katy-perry-logo-png-clipart.jpg` (clip-art site) | Not an official file; artists don't publish brand kits. |
| Kesha | `Keshas_new_logo.png` | Not an official file. |

Replace any of these with an official file from the brand's own media kit
when one becomes available.
